## Where it began

Coffee is a daily ritual for me and a livelihood for millions of farmers.
That imbalance stayed with me during my AI studies: most of the attention goes
to the cup, very little to the plant.

## Design principles

- **Start from the farmer's view.** Photos taken on an ordinary phone, in real
  light, are the primary input, not lab-quality images.
- **Assist, don't decide.** The system suggests what might be happening and
  why, and leaves the decision to the person who knows the field.
- **Be honest about uncertainty.** A confident wrong answer about crop disease
  is worse than "I'm not sure, check these leaves again in a few days".
- **Work with poor connectivity.** Anything that only works with fast internet
  won't work where it is needed most.

## What I'm learning

Building a model is the smaller part of the problem. Framing the right
question, finding representative data, and presenting results that people can
trust and act on are much harder, and much more interesting.
