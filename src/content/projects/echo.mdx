## Where it began

I noticed that I kept writing notes like "remember this next time you're
here", and then forgetting them, because nothing reminded me when I was
actually there. Echo is an attempt to let the place do the reminding.

## The hard part is restraint

Technically, triggering a notification when someone enters an area is not
difficult. Deciding whether it *should* fire is much harder.

- A note that arrives at the wrong moment is noise.
- Too many notes in one area become a list, and lists get ignored.
- Location permissions are personal. The app has to earn them and explain
  exactly why it needs them.

The design direction became: fewer, quieter notifications, and notes that feel
discovered rather than delivered.

## Still open

Echo is an experiment rather than a product. The open questions are more
interesting to me than the code: how should a memory feel when a device
brings it back, and when is it kinder to let a place stay silent?
